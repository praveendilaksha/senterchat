# Senter Fest — Frequently Asked Questions

Please review the following important information before purchasing your ticket.

### 🎟️ Ticket & Entry

*Q: Is the ticket valid for one day or both days?*
*A:* Your ticket is valid for *both days of Senter Fest*.

*Q: Is re-entry allowed?*
*A:* No. *Re-entry is not permitted.* Each ticket allows *one entry per day*.

*Q: What time do the gates open?*
*A:* The gates open at *4:00 PM* on both days.

---

### 👥 Age Requirements

*Q: Is there an age limit?*
*A:* Yes. Ticket holders must be *16 years or older*.

Guests *under 16 years of age* must be accompanied by a *parent or legal guardian*, who must purchase the ticket on their behalf and accompany them to the event.

---

### 🚫 Prohibited Items

*Q: What items are not allowed inside the festival?*
*A:* For the safety and security of all guests, the following items are strictly prohibited:

* Drugs and illegal substances
* Cigarettes and smoking products
* Weapons or dangerous objects
* Outside liquids and beverages
* Outside food
* Alcohol

All guests are subject to security checks at the entrance.

---

### 🚗 Parking

*Q: Is parking available at the event?*
*A:* Dedicated event parking is *not available*.

Parking within the Port City area, where applicable, is *at the vehicle owner's own risk*. Please follow all applicable parking regulations and restrictions.

---

### 💳 Refunds & Tickets

*Q: Are refunds available?*
*A:* No. *All ticket sales are final and non-refundable.*

In the event that Senter Fest is officially *cancelled by the organisers*, the full ticket value will be refunded according to the organiser's refund process.

*Q: Can I upgrade my GA ticket to VIP?*
*A:* No. *GA tickets cannot be upgraded to VIP.*

*Q: Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?*
*A:* No. *Tickets are non-transferable* and may only be used by the original ticket holder.

*Q: How can I make sure my ticket is official?*
*A:* Only tickets issued through the official *Senter Fest ticketing channel — [tickets@lfg-entertainment.com](mailto:tickets@lfg-entertainment.com) — are considered valid and official*.

Please be cautious when purchasing tickets from third parties. The organisers are not responsible for tickets purchased through unauthorised sources.

---

### 🌧️ Weather

*Q: What happens if it rains?*
*A:* *We dance in the rain! 🌧️🔥*

Senter Fest is an outdoor experience, so please come prepared for changing weather conditions. Unless the event is officially cancelled by the organisers, the festival will continue as scheduled.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notice

By purchasing a ticket and attending Senter Fest, you agree to comply with the event rules, security procedures, and instructions issued by the organisers and event security team.

*See you at Senter Fest! 🎶🔥*
